Gestures

  • Frame with both hands: spread thumb and index finger on each hand. The shape between your four fingertips gets a filter. Flip one hand to twist it into two triangles.
  • Pinch both hands closed while framing (the shape disappears): next box filter. Open your fingers again to see it.
  • Snap your fingers: right hand = next camera filter, left hand = previous (watches your fingers first; the mic helps catch fast, blurry snaps)
  • Swipe an open hand left to right across the screen: clear everything (fog, writing, camera filter)
  • Open your mouth and blow (a "hooo" breath, not talking): fog the screen. The mic listens for the blowing sound; without a mic, an open mouth alone works. It clears itself a few seconds after you stop
  • Point one index finger (up or at the camera): write in the fog
  • Open palm over the fog: wipe it away like a misted window

Keys

Click the video or press Space to pause tracking (effects freeze in place) and again to resume.

1-9 box filters, F next box filter, G next camera filter, C clear fog, S hand skeleton, P photo, H help
